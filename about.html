Why it is free

No gatekeeping

For as long as I've used computers and phones, I've wondered why anyone charges for a program when it could simply be free. I know the usual answer: people pour real work into what they build, sometimes hours, sometimes days, and that effort deserves something back. That's fair, and I don't argue with it. But at FP Systems we don't need payment. We put hard work into every program and still choose to give it away. Use them, fork them, change them, do whatever you want with them.
