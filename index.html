File limits

  • Each file must be no larger than 2 MB.
  • The complete ZIP file must be no larger than 5 MB.
  • Up to 1 index.html file.
  • Up to 3 other HTML files.
  • Up to 3 CSS files.
  • Up to 3 JavaScript files.
  • Up to 10 image files, such as PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF.
  • Folders are allowed; files inside them count toward the limits.
  • No videos, including embedded or iframe videos and YouTube videos.

Submission rules

By submitting files to furpri.de, you agree:

  • No illegal content or content that facilitates illegal activity.
  • No attempts to evade, bypass, or exploit these rules or the site’s safeguards.
  • No graphic gore or pornographic content, including hentai or sexually explicit material.
  • No content that could expose users to serious legal or safety risks.
  • These rules also apply to embedded and linked material.
Submissions may be rejected, disabled, or removed at any time, with or without prior warning or notice. Future submissions may also be restricted.
