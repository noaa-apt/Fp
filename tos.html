A. Submitting

1a. Pack your static website files into one ZIP file and email it to 5555525@morke.ru. You attach the ZIP yourself.

2a. Each file must be no larger than 2 MB. The whole ZIP must be no larger than 5 MB.

3a. Up to 1 index.html file and up to 3 other HTML files.

4a. Up to 3 CSS files and up to 3 JavaScript files.

5a. Up to 10 image files (PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP or GIF).

6a. Folders are allowed. Files inside folders count toward all the limits above.

7a. No videos. This includes embedded videos, iframe videos and YouTube videos.

8a. Do not hide files to get around the limits, for example by putting extra files inside another archive inside your ZIP.

B. Static only, no backends

1b. Your page must be plain static files. No server-side code of any kind.

2b. No backends. Your page must not depend on a backend or hosted platform for its logic or data, for example Netlify, Vercel, Firebase, Supabase, serverless functions, or similar services.

3b. No forms or scripts that send data to a backend or any server.

4b. JavaScript is fine for things that run in the visitor's browser. It must not send visitor data to anyone.

5b. Your page must be its own content. Pages that only redirect to, mirror, or wrap another site in a frame are not allowed.

C. Content

1c. No illegal content, and no content that helps people do illegal things.

2c. No graphic gore.

3c. No pornographic or sexually explicit content, including hentai.

4c. No sexual content involving minors, ever, in any form (drawn, written or otherwise).

5c. No content that could expose visitors to serious legal or safety risks.

D. No discrimination

1d. No discrimination, hate or harassment against people or groups because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or anything similar.

2d. No promoting hate groups, hate symbols or hateful ideologies.

3d. No threats, doxxing, or targeting of individuals.

4d. This applies to every kind of page and every part of it: text, images, page titles, file names, code comments, and anything else in your files.

E. Security and honesty

1e. No malware, exploits, cryptominers, or anything that downloads or runs unwanted things on a visitor's computer.

2e. No phishing, fake login pages, or pretending to be another person, company or organization.

3e. No tracking, fingerprinting or secretly collecting information about visitors.

4e. Do not pretend to be furpri.de or its administrators.

F. Embedded and linked material

1f. All of these rules also apply to embedded and linked material.

2f. If your page links to or loads something that breaks a rule, that counts as your page breaking the rule.

3f. You are responsible for everything in your files, including code, fonts, images and text that come from someone else.

G. Fair play

1g. Do not try to evade, bypass or exploit these rules or the site's safeguards.

2g. Do not look for loopholes in the wording. The spirit of the rules counts as much as the exact words.

3g. Do not resubmit a removed page under a new name or email address to get around a removal or restriction.

4g. Do not spam submissions.

H. Page quality

1h. Your page must work on its own. Use relative links and include everything it needs in your ZIP, within the limits.

2h. No empty, placeholder or test pages. Your page must have real content.

3h. No pages made only to advertise, spam, or push search engine rankings.

4h. Page titles and descriptions must not be misleading about what is on the page.

5h. Your page must not hog the visitor's computer. No endless loops, crypto-style heavy computing, or scripts built to freeze or crash the browser.

6h. Your page must open in a normal web browser with no plugins or extra software.

7h. No pop-ups, forced redirects, or tricks that stop visitors from leaving or going back.

I. Ownership and copyright

1i. Only submit things you made or have permission to publish.

2i. No pirated material, cracked software, or links to it.

3i. No personal information about other people without their permission.

4i. Keep the licenses and credits of any third-party code, fonts or images you include.

5i. If someone makes a valid ownership or copyright claim about your page, it may be removed.

J. Enforcement

1j. Administrators decide whether a page follows the rules. Their decision is final.

2j. Submissions may be rejected, disabled or removed at any time, with or without prior warning or notice.

3j. If an administrator decides your page is unacceptable, or sees that there is no future for your page here, they can and will remove it, warning or not.

4j. Future submissions from you may be restricted.

5j. These rules may change. The current version is always on this page.
